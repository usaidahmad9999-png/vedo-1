a letter for you

My Dear Warda

Dear Vedo,

Today the world celebrates the day a beautiful soul was born — and honestly, I think the world got lucky. Because you, Warda, are the kind of person who walks into a room and somehow makes it warmer, louder, and a whole lot more fun.

Do you remember our Ludo nights? You'd be so serious, tapping the dice, plotting your next move like a chess grandmaster — and then I'd accidentally roll a six and you'd throw a tantrum that would make a toddler proud. We'd argue, laugh, argue some more, and then start a new round like nothing happened.

Our group chats were a whole universe. Hours of talking about absolutely nothing, roasting each other without mercy, sending the most unhinged stickers at 2 AM. You always had the perfect savage sticker for every situation. I still have a folder full of them. I'm not deleting any of them. Ever.

We used to make jokes on each other so hard that sometimes I forget if you're my sister or my best friend. Maybe you're both. Maybe you're the best of both worlds.

And through all of it — the laughing, the teasing, the random sticker wars, the Ludo arguments — you've been there. Always. And that means more to me than I probably say enough.

I pray for you, Vedo. Every single day. For your happiness, your peace, your dreams. I hope the universe gives you everything your heart whispers about.

May Allah keep you safe, keep you smiling, and keep that beautiful light of yours burning bright. May every door you knock on open. May every prayer you whisper find its way to the sky.

Happy Birthday, Warda. Happy Birthday, Vedo. The world is better with you in it, and I'm better with you in mine.

— Your favourite (and only) brother, who will always roast you but also always have your back 🌹
